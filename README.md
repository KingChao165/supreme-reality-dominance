# Supreme Reality Dominance
A modern Minecraft modpack about survival, space, and <ins>scope creep.</ins>

### Disclaimer: the yapping below is a placeholder while I figure out how to actually make this pack a reality. It's very self-indulgent, I'll fix it later.

**I love Minecraft.**
This should surprise literally no one: I'm autistic, creative, and I'm a zoomer. Anyone who meets even just 1 of those 3 criteria probably either plays Minecraft or has seen so much of MCYT that they may as well have killed the Ender Dragon themselves (not really, but close enough).

But, **I also love Minecraft mods!**
Been there since I could mod the game, but even before then I dreamed of being able to go to space on my Xbox 360 edition version of the game as a kid. Galacticraft, iChun's Portal Gun, the Halo mods, emerald tools and armor, and eventually OptiFine when @tentodan showed it to me. Big fan of the mods, from then to the 1.12.2 packs of the ChaoCraft days, all the way to now in a post-Create world with the All The Mods pack being, in my opinion: the flagship modpack, with custom quests, cross-mod compatibility and integration, and...it runs well! With over 400 mods it's pretty PC friendly, taking advantage of Sodium forks and optimization mods that probably make up 15-20% of the mod count alone.
So yeah, I love mods.
And I've been making custom packs for years, mostly just cobbled up collections for friends or servers with not much cross-compat other than that which was plug and play.

About 3-4 years ago, I started a project of making the ultimate Chao Craft modpack. Even if I never made it public with a server, just to have for myself and my friends would be such a game changer (literally). This pack was originally codenamed Power Creep, named after the server for which it would be hosted on: ChaoCraft Power Creep. The name is a play on the idea that a big part of balancing a modpack (or any game, for that matter) is not giving the player too much power too quickly, or else it makes the entire game trivial and boring. The double meaning is that it also refers to electrical power, since the pack was largely tech-focused. I don't need to get into the history of tech mods, you know what's up. Mekanism FTW btw.
Power Creep would evolve over years of failed attempts and reflection on the core design principles of Minecraft, turning into the one and only:

Supreme Reality Dominance (S.R.D.) is all about two things: improving Vanilla progression, and introducing technology (and space) that scales from prim to techno-godhood. 
It combines aspects of factory automation games like Factorio, Satisfactory, and especially ASTRONEER (and maybe just a smidge of Cookie Clicker) with my favorite survival open world games: Subnautica (mostly this one), RUST, Slime Rancher, Starbound, and it tops the whole thing off with a pinch of my other favorite games outside of those genres: that being Outer Wilds and Portal 2.
It is a very "me" pack. It is the ultimate expression of what I want from a game, crystallized into a complete experience that can bridge the gap between fun and awe. Minecraft is so close to already being this in it of itself, SRD just takes the logical next step while also pandering to my materialist transhumanist existentialist heart.
Minecraft has magic. SRD has technology that is so advanced it's indistinguishable from magic on the surface, but the two could not be further apart under the hood.
So yeah: the Nether is a place under the Earth (overworld) now.

Space exists and holds resources you cannot get anywhere else.
Enchanting is GONE, replaced with material cost upgrades and more tool tiers (like Raspberry Flavoured did it, more on that later).
All materials are made up of matter, which is energy + information. Store that information in a sophisticated computer (we have those), pay for the cost with power, and your storage capacity can be nigh-infinite.
The world is both harsher and calmer than normal. Hostile mobs are rarer and fewer among them under normal circumstances, but now you must worry about your other biological needs: thirst, temperature, and sleep. Don't worry, once you get advanced enough these things become trivial, just like in our real world.
Landmasses are farther apart, separated by great seas and lakes. The Earth now has a biosphere with a sufficient amount of water, and more life under that water for you to explore and exploit.
Materials come in their chemical-accurate notations, and can be purified for more applications. The air you breathe can also be a resource (this also works on planets with other gasses in their atmosphere, like Venus).]
I haven't decided yet if I'm gonna add the mod that lets you cook crystal meth in the game, but if you can: break bad!
And the biggest thing of all: NO MORE ITEM LOSS!!!

Despawning items are getting seriously extended. All explosions and broken blocks drop their items. Conversion is always 100% accurate or will make waste products instead. The only energy loss I accept is heat/radiation. Matter will be conserved. Praise Newton.
I invite anyone who plays my pack when it is out to play it however they like. The adventurers will have their landscapes, the artists will have so many more colors and materials to choose from, the urbanites can create machinations unknown to mortal men, the rustic survivors will have marshmallows and campfires, and YOU. AND I. WILL HAVE THE WORLD AT OUR HANDS.

If only you are willing to do what it takes to supremely dominate your reality.
That's the pitch.
